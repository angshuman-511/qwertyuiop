Every material
has a story.

From traditional techniques to

01

Rich · layered · timeless

Oil & Acrylic Paintings

Slow-building layers, luminous colour and expressive brushwork. Oil and acrylic painting offers depth and texture that rewards a closer look — from grand abstracts to intimate portraits.

Explore paintings ↗
02

Observed · composed · evocative

Photography

Fine-art photographs turn fleeting light, places and everyday details into images worth living with. From architecture to landscapes, each frame captures a decisive moment.

View photography ↗
03

Human · tactile · unique

Handmade & Craft

Textiles, ceramics, paper art, woodwork and mixed-media objects — each carries the marks and personality of its maker. Every piece is one of a kind.

Meet handmade art ↗
04

Ephemeral · intricate · elemental

Sand Art

From elaborate beach sculptures to bottled sand art, this ancient medium turns grains into breathtaking compositions. Impermanence is part of the beauty.

Discover sand art ↗
05

Bold · tactile · versatile

Canvas Art

From acrylic abstracts to minimal compositions, canvas brings an expressive surface and a sense of presence to a space. Bold colours meet thoughtful strokes.

Discover canvas ↗
06

Experimental · layered · eclectic

Mixed Media & Other

Collage, assemblage, digital-physical hybrids and unconventional materials. When artists break rules, the most surprising art emerges.

Explore mixed media ↗
